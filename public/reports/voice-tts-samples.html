Pilot voices: listen

Eight readbacks taken from the game's own phraseology, each read by the four voices of Paradee-8M v1.2, the 8.5 MB model that is meant to run in your browser, and by Kokoro-82M, the 82M-parameter model it was distilled from. Each clip comes dry and through the radio effect the game will use.

Paradee int8 ONNX, CPUKokoro-82M, PyTorch CPURadio: 300–3400 Hz band-pass, soft compression, faint hiss, squelch clicks

Measurements (speed, word error rate, browser latency) are in the pilot voice report.

A big model for comparison

Audio8-TTS-Realistic-Air (Edge0, Apache-2.0) is a 0.6B-parameter DualAR model with a 44.1 kHz codec, 2.5 GB of weights. It is far too big for the browser; it is here as a sense of what a large model sounds like, and as a possible offline generator of training audio.
