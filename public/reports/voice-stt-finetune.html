How to read this

  • 16 recordings is a small sample. 1.7 % against 2.4 % is one word. The coverage test (805 lines) is the firmer number, and it is synthetic speech.
  • Real radio is still hard: 17 to 23 %. That audio is noisy, includes pilots and has Czech and Swiss place names. The game hears a controller on a headset, which is closer to ATCOSIM (1.8 %).
  • Speed is unchanged: the model is the same size. About 0.5 to 0.9 s per transmission on the server.
  • Licence: both rounds include recordings licensed for non-commercial use only (UWB-ATCC, ATCOSIM). A commercial launch needs a version trained on the synthetic speech alone.
  • Missing accents: East Asian, Middle Eastern, Australian and African English. No voice engine here speaks them well; recordings from real players are the fix.
