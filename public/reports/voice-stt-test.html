Speech-to-text test

Hold the button (or the Space bar), say the line, let go. Your recording goes to our own recognisers, the ones the game uses: Parakeet and Whisper on our server, and if you tick it, the small model that runs in this browser. Google is not used. You see what each one heard, which words it got wrong, and how long it took after you let go.

Words wrong = word error rate against the line you read, with numbers compared digit by digit ("niner" = 9, "two seven left" = "27L"). Time = from letting go of the button to the text arriving, including the upload; the game keeps recording for a quarter of a second after you let go so the last word is not cut, and that is included. "Model" is the recogniser's own time. Both server engines are asked at once here, so they share the server and each is a little slower than in the game, which asks Parakeet alone and Whisper only when Parakeet's text is not understood. Lines you say your own way are shown but not scored.
