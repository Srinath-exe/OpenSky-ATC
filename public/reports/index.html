 resultsThe command model learns free wording

Overnight, on CPU: everyday wording and hard cases, an independent exam and a stress exam, voiced speech end to end. 40 to 51 of 60 sent right on the independent exam, fewer wrong sends on every set, live as 2m-free-v1. Plus: the pilot voice needs no training, and no GPU job is needed.

 resultsFree wording

270 transmissions said the way a person talks, not the way the book prints them: dangerous misreadings found and fixed, 94 % on the tuned set, 69 % on unseen wordings, and what that means for the command model.

 resultsPilots against a careless controller

Look-ahead, party line, crew personalities and a self-play arena: an adversary made 628 controller mistakes in a running airport, the pilots caught 627, with no false alarms under a good controller.
