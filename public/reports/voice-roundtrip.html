Voice round trip

Hold the button, give the command, let go. Everything after that is automatic, as in the game: our speech-to-text writes down what you said while you are still talking, the game reads it as a command (its rules, then the small command model in this browser), the aircraft's pilot reads it back, and you hear the pilot's voice. Every step is timed, and every round trip is saved so we can see where the time goes.

Times run from the moment you let go of the button to the moment the pilot's voice starts. In the game the pilot also waits before answering, as real pilots do (2 to 4 s in the air, 4 to 8 s on the ground; Settings, "Pilot response delay"), and the voice is prepared during that wait, so there it adds about 0.1 s instead of the pilot voice time shown here. That wait is left out here so the machine's own time is visible.
