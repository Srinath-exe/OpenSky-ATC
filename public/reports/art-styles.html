SFO in six art styles

The same view of the game's own 3D SFO (taxiway W, runways 28L/28R, the terminal behind), re-drawn in six styles at day, dusk and night. Each style is a single screen pass over the real render, so any of them could be built into the game for real. Click an image for full size.

The game today

What the 3D map looks like now, for comparison.

01

Anime cel

Saturated flat colour in four tones, ink outlines on every edge, a painted sky. Closest to a hand-drawn anime background.

02

Blueprint

White linework on drafting blue with a grid; at night the lights are drawn in amber. Reads like an engineering drawing of the field.

03

16-bit pixel art

Chunky 4 px pixels, a small palette with ordered dithering, dark outlines, a banded sky. A retro game look.

04

Watercolour

Brush-smoothed shapes, pigment pooling at the edges, paper grain and a soft vignette.

05

Neon synthwave

A dark world drawn in glowing edges, magenta near you to cyan far away, with scanlines.

06

Cinematic

The realistic direction: bloom on the lights, a teal and orange film grade, contrast, vignette and grain.
