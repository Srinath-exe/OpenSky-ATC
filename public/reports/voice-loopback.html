Pilot voice loopback

A pilot line is spoken by the game's neural pilot voice (Paradee-8M, running in this browser), then written back by our server's recognisers. Each line shows how long every step took and which words came back wrong. Nothing here uses Google.

The voice model loads on the first line (about 12 MB, cached afterwards).

Words wrong = word error rate against the text the voice was given, with numbers compared digit by digit ("niner" = "nine" = 9, "fife" = 5, "two seven left" = "27L"). Time to first sound = from the line arriving to the first clause being ready to play (the rest is synthesised while it plays). The server times are the recogniser's own time on our server, not counting the upload.
